Labels are sized for a US Letter sheet of 30 labels, 3 across (Avery 5160 or the same size). Plain paper also works: cut them out. In the print window choose 100% scale (no "fit to page"). "Skip" leaves blanks at the start, so a part-used sheet can be reused. The barcode is Code 128 and holds the item's barcode value, which is its SKU.

Test a label

Scan a printed label with the USB scanner or the camera. The item should appear below. This is the same lookup the Point of Sale and Inventory pages use.
